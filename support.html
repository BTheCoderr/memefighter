Online room not connecting?

The free online server may take time to wake up after inactivity. Wait briefly and retry. Confirm both players are using the same six-character room code and that both tap READY. Rooms are temporary and may reset if the server restarts.

Game controls

Use the on-screen directional pad and attack buttons on phones. Computer players have Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty settings. For online matches, the room host and guest should use their own device. The current online beta supports casual private two-player rooms, not ranked tournaments or cash matches.
