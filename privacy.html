Your fights. Your privacy.

Meme Fighter is a casual fighting game with offline computer opponents and optional private online rooms. The current beta does not require an account, offer paid purchases, or include third-party advertising.

What the game stores

Offline wins and losses are saved on your device using local storage where supported. When you join an online room, the game stores a temporary room code and a random seat-reconnection token in browser session storage. Online rooms and combat snapshots are held in the game server's working memory while the room exists. They can be lost after inactivity or server restarts.

Online multiplayer connections

Joining an online room connects your device to the hosted Meme Fighter server over HTTPS and encrypted WebSockets. It processes game actions, room codes, connection status and temporary combat state to operate the match. The hosting provider may also process technical connection information such as IP addresses, timestamps and request logs for delivery, security and diagnostics. Retention of infrastructure logs depends on the hosting provider's practices.

Sharing and outside services

If you choose to share an invitation, your device's share interface creates a link containing the room code. The recipient may open that link in their browser. We don't add your name or address to that invitation. The game uses a third-party hosting provider for online matches. We do not currently sell gameplay information or use it for behavioral advertising.

Your choices

You can play against the CPU without connecting to the online server. You can clear device-local game stats through your browser's site-data controls or by removing the installed application data as supported by your device. Leaving a private online room ends participation; the server may retain transient information until the room expires or the service restarts.

Changes and questions

This describes the present beta's technical behavior and may change when accounts, matchmaking, analytics or other features are added. We will update this page when those features materially affect privacy. For questions, use the support page.
